Cron Reminder: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Cron Reminder is an Android app that shows notifications on schedules you write as cron expressions. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your device, and nothing is collected or shared.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage information. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. It does not access the network.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves on your device only:

  • the reminders you create: their titles and cron expressions;
  • the notifications it has scheduled for them;
  • your theme preference (system, light or dark).

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by removing reminders in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup under Google's terms; the app itself does not send it anywhere.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the exact minute you scheduled.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration and keep the device awake briefly: to deliver the notification.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party services that receive your data. Google Play, which distributes the app, handles installs and updates under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising, this policy will be updated before that change ships, with a new effective date at the top of this page.
